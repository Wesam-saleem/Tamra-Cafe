Our Story

Where the Date Farm
Meets the Coffee Cup

Tamra Coffee House was born from a simple but powerful idea: to bring the richness of Jordanian Medjool dates from our family farm directly to your cup and plate. Every sip, every bite tells the story of our land.

We believe that great coffee and exceptional food begin at the source. Our farm-to-table philosophy means everything we serve — from our signature date lattes to our handcrafted desserts — is made with premium ingredients sourced directly from our own date farm.
